# Code of Conduct

Blitz is built and used by a small, high-trust community: licensed operators, security researchers, and contributors working on offensive security tooling. That context shapes this document. We expect the same rigor and good faith here that we expect in how Blitz itself is used against a target — nothing here overrides the [License Agreement](./LICENSE.md) or the reporting process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md); this covers how people treat each other.

---

## Our Standard

Participants in this project — in the repository, issue tracker, discussions, Discord/Slack, or any other project space — are expected to:

- **Engage in good faith.** Assume competence and good intent, especially in technical disagreements about heuristics, capability-gating logic, or architecture.
- **Be precise.** This is a tool that executes real actions against real, often fragile hardware. Vague bug reports, exaggerated severity claims, or unverified "exploit" claims waste triage time and erode trust. Say what you tested, what you observed, and what you didn't verify.
- **Respect scope.** Don't use project spaces to solicit, share, or coordinate unauthorized use of Blitz against systems the requester doesn't have documented authorization to test. See the Responsible Use Reminder in SECURITY.md — the same line applies here.
- **Disagree without contempt.** Pointed technical criticism is welcome. Personal attacks, mockery of skill level, or dismissiveness toward newer contributors are not.
- **Handle sensitive material carefully.** Don't paste live credentials, real target details, unredacted findings, or another party's data into public issues, PRs, or chat — even as a "for example." Treat it the way you'd treat a live pentest report.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language directed at any participant.
- Publicly disclosing a vulnerability in Blitz outside the coordinated process in SECURITY.md.
- Sharing license credentials, repository access, or container builds in violation of the seat-scope terms in the License Agreement, or asking others to do so.
- Soliciting or bragging about use of Blitz against targets without documented authorization.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews; doxxing; impersonation; or sharing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make a participant feel unsafe engaging with the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, private vulnerability reports, and any official community channels — and to any individual representing the project in public spaces (e.g., speaking about Blitz at a conference or online under the project's name).

It does not extend to a Licensee's independent use of Blitz against authorized targets, which is governed instead by the License Agreement and SECURITY.md's Responsible Use Reminder.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation:

- For conduct issues unrelated to security: contact the maintainers at `conduct@[yourdomain].com` *(replace with your real contact)*, or open a private report if your platform supports it.
- For anything that overlaps with a security vulnerability or evidence of unauthorized use: follow the reporting process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead — those reports are handled through that channel, not this one.

Reports are handled confidentially. Include what happened, where, when, and who was involved, along with any relevant links or screenshots. We'll acknowledge receipt within a reasonable timeframe and follow up on next steps.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take action they deem appropriate in response to any behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. This can include:

| Response | When it applies |
|---|---|
| **Private warning** | First-time, lower-severity issues |
| **Temporary restriction** | Repeated issues after a warning (e.g., time-limited ban from issues/PRs) |
| **Permanent removal** | Severe violations, or continued violation after restriction — from project spaces, and where applicable, revocation of repository access consistent with the License Agreement |

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face consequences determined by other project leadership.
